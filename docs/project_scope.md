# Project Scope

## Project Goal

Build a polished end-to-end analytics and machine learning project that helps Salifort Motors understand employee attrition and identify practical HR retention actions.

## Stakeholders

- HR leadership
- People analytics team
- Department managers
- Workforce planning and operations leaders

## In Scope

- Data cleaning and validation.
- Exploratory analysis of attrition patterns.
- Feature engineering for HR-relevant predictors.
- Binary classification model training.
- Model comparison and evaluation.
- Business recommendations for retention strategy.
- Portfolio-quality documentation and repository structure.

## Out of Scope

- Production model deployment.
- Real-time employee monitoring.
- Automated HR decision-making.
- Legal or compliance approval for HR technology use.
- Validation on private company HR systems.

## Assumptions

- The dataset is representative enough for capstone analysis.
- The `left` field is a reliable attrition label.
- Historical HR patterns are useful for identifying potential retention risks.
- Model outputs will be reviewed by humans before any action is taken.

## Risks and Limitations

- Some features may reflect late-stage attrition behavior rather than early warning signals.
- The dataset may not fully represent real company complexity.
- Model performance may decline on new or current HR data.
- Employee data requires careful governance and ethical use.

## Success Criteria

The project is successful if it demonstrates:

- Clear business framing.
- Clean and reproducible analysis.
- Strong EDA and modeling workflow.
- Honest model evaluation.
- Practical HR recommendations.
- Professional GitHub presentation suitable for recruiters.
